## Long Description

SeeVido AI is an all-in-one creative platform for turning text prompts, photos, and reference media into videos, images, music, and visual effects. Its video workflows include text-to-video, image-to-video, photo-to-video, reference-to-video, and video-to-video creation. Users can select from multiple available generation models and control settings such as aspect ratio, resolution, duration, and output mode from a unified interface.

The platform also includes AI image generation and editing tools for tasks such as cartoon conversion, headshots, photo restoration, face enhancement, hairstyle changes, clothing changes, and other visual transformations. Music tools and ready-made video effects help creators develop short-form content, campaign concepts, social posts, product visuals, and creative experiments without moving between many separate services.

SeeVido AI provides free credits and selected free tools for new users, alongside credit-based subscriptions and one-time credit packages. The current pricing page displays 40 credits for new users, 20 daily check-in credits, and five daily uses of free tools. Paid access can provide additional credits and expanded usage options according to the selected package. SeeVido AI is suitable for content creators, marketers, designers, small businesses, educators, and anyone who wants a practical workspace for producing visual and audio content with generative AI.
